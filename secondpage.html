SieslaHora is committed to protecting your personal data and processing it lawfully, fairly and transparently, in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you interact with SieslaHora, we may collect the following personal information:

  • First name and last name;
  • Email address;
  • Date of birth;
  • Gender, where provided;
  • Information relating to your consent and marketing preferences;
  • Information relating to your participation in promotions, competitions or offers.

We only collect personal data that is relevant to the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

How We Use Your Personal Data

Your personal data may be processed for the following purposes:

  • To administer your participation in promotions, competitions, prize draws and voucher campaigns operated by SieslaHora;
  • To contact you in relation to your participation in a promotion or to provide information about vouchers, offers or prizes;
  • To send SieslaHora newsletters, offers and promotional communications where you have provided the appropriate consent;
  • Where you have provided separate and specific consent, to facilitate promotional communications from third-party companies identified or described in the relevant consent information;
  • To manage and record your marketing preferences and requests to withdraw consent;
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary.

Lawful Basis for Processing

Depending on the purpose of the processing, we rely on one or more of the following lawful bases under the UK GDPR:

  • Consent: where you have actively opted in to receive marketing communications or where your data is to be shared with third parties for promotional purposes;
  • Contract or steps prior to entering into a contract: where processing is necessary to administer your participation in a promotion or provide a service you have requested;
  • Legal obligation: where we are required to process or retain information to comply with applicable law;
  • Legitimate interests: where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests and those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

Marketing Communications

Where required by applicable law, we will only send direct marketing communications by email where you have provided valid consent or another lawful basis applies.

Marketing consent is obtained through a clear affirmative action, such as actively selecting an opt-in checkbox. Marketing consent is not obtained through pre-ticked boxes or silence.

You can withdraw your consent to receive marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe mechanism included in our emails or by contacting us using the details provided below.

Sharing Your Personal Data

We may share personal data with:

  • Service providers acting as data processors on our behalf, including hosting providers, email delivery providers and technical service providers;
  • Third-party commercial partners where you have provided specific consent for your personal data to be shared for promotional purposes;
  • Professional advisers, regulators, public authorities or other parties where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Where personal data is shared with third parties for their own direct marketing purposes, the relevant organisations or the categories of organisations will be identified or described in the information provided to you at the time of obtaining consent.

Personal data will not be publicly disclosed.

Categories of Third-Party Partners

Depending on the consent you provide, your personal data may be shared with independent commercial partners operating in areas such as:

  • Supermarkets and grocery services;
  • Retail and e-commerce;
  • Energy and utilities;
  • Telecommunications;
  • Travel and leisure;
  • Financial services;
  • Health and wellbeing;
  • Entertainment and lifestyle.

Where required, specific third-party organisations will be identified in the relevant consent request before your data is shared with them.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and to comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

  • Data relating to participation in promotions may normally be retained for up to 12 months after the relevant promotion has ended, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
  • Data processed for marketing purposes may be retained until you withdraw your consent, subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
  • Where personal data has been shared with independent third parties, those organisations may retain and process the data in accordance with their own privacy notices and applicable legal requirements.
